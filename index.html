11 Five-year narrative — the turning points
2021 — Bounce-back
UR fell from 23.5% (April 2020 peak) to 6.38% by year-end. Startups, edtech, logistics drove hiring. Gig platforms absorbed surplus labour. Remote work unlocked Tier-2/3 talent pools.
2022 — The boom
IT added 6L jobs. IIT placement hit 88–96%. Startup funding peaked at $36B. Attrition hit 21.4% — India's Great Resignation chapter. Average hike peaked at 9.7%.
2023 — The correction
36,000+ IT layoffs. VC funding winter ($9.6B). Campus placements began declining. Edtech imploded. IIT Guwahati placements dipped to 71%. CMIE UR touched 9.2%.
2024 — Structural shift
AI-driven productivity decouples revenue from headcount. GCC hiring accelerates at 30–40% premium to legacy services. Traditional IT enters margin squeeze — TCS cuts begin.
2025 — The reset
Attrition stabilises at 16.2% near pre-Covid norms. Average hike moderates to 9.1% as margin pressure persists. Female LFPR hits 35.3% — all-time high. Youth UR remains sticky at 15.2%.
