08Five-year narrative — the turning points
2021 — Bounce-back
UR fell from 23.5% (April 2020 CMIE peak) to 6.38% by year-end. Startups, logistics drove hiring. Gig platforms absorbed surplus labour. Remote work unlocked Tier-2 talent pools.
2022 — The boom
IT added 6L jobs. IIT placement hit 88–96%. Startup funding peaked at $36B. Attrition hit 21.4% — India's Great Resignation. Average hike peaked at 9.7%. FLFPR: 32.8%.
2023 — The correction
36,000+ IT layoffs. VC funding winter ($9.6B). Campus placements began declining. Edtech imploded. IIT Guwahati placements dipped to 71%. Annual UR fell to 3.5% (usual status).
2024 — Structural decoupling
AI-driven productivity decouples revenue from headcount for the first time. GCC hiring accelerates at 30–40% premium. Traditional IT enters margin squeeze. TCS cuts 23K. Record low annual UR: 3.2%.
2025–26 — The new normal
Attrition stabilises at 16.2%. Average hike 9.1%. Female LFPR hits 41.7% — all-time annual high. Monthly CWS UR drifts to 5.1% in March 2026. Youth unemployment 15.2% remains structural challenge. GCC economy absorbs premium talent displaced from services firms.
